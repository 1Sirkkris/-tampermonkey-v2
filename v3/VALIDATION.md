## What is not proven

No live Amazon mutation was submitted during this rebuild. DOM fixtures do not validate Amazon's deployed markup, authentication policy, iframe restrictions, third-party storage partitioning, backend contracts or actual inventory results. Those require the user’s controlled tests and OBS evidence. UNKNOWN remains a verification barrier even if a network error looks recoverable.

Native Measurement history must cover the requested 30-day window before reporting NO. An incomplete page or unavailable authenticated native worker produces AUTH/RETRY, not a negative result.
