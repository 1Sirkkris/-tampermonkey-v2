## Immediate recovery feedback contract

PRESERVE: V2 panel-local grey loading overlay and wheel, shown synchronously when the waiting Predicant destination is rescanned. Existing three areas, scanner focus, Stop and native operational sequence stay unchanged. Native progress keeps the overlay through destination recovery; awaiting a deliberate destination scan or date shows the underlying controls.

FAILURES: no silent rescan while a native command is being accepted; no duplicate destination controls during the same recovery; no false "confirmed" label before native evidence; no spinner left after rejection, Stop, UNKNOWN or disposal.

STATE: the console owns only immediate pending feedback, cleared by native recovery progress or run settlement. Native Sideline remains the sole operation owner. An old waiting-state notification cannot clear a newly pending rescan. No new polling, waits, native requests or timeout/retry owner.

SUCCESS/UNKNOWN: feedback never proves movement. Existing typed native responses, journals and UNKNOWN barriers govern outcomes. Failed destination close reopens the scan gate; uncertain close quarantines and ends feedback without replay.

RESET: Stop removes loading feedback immediately while the native submitted operation settles under its existing owner. Further scans cannot restart it. Clear awaits settlement and retains unresolved rows; dispose removes owned UI/events. Loading is non-intercepting and scanner input/Stop remain usable.

DEPENDENCIES: existing exact-peer native bridge/progress. Offline source and generated installer tests plus real native-client fixtures; deployed scanner timing, appearance and Predicant outcomes remain live gates.
